A small language model writes an answer from the passages' live text, on this device: it downloads once (several hundred MB, then cached). The question and the passages' text go nowhere else.

    How it works: the index holds, for each passage, its embedding (bge-small-en-v1.5), the article, the section number, and the passage's first twelve words. It was built with cognee and is stored as Lance tables; no passage text is in the bundle. Your question is embedded here with the same model (transformers.js), and nanolance's WebAssembly reader fetches only the nearest partitions' rows by HTTP Range from this static site.

    Searching contacts Wikipedia. Unlike the offline page, each result's text is requested from en.wikipedia.org by its pinned revision and section, at most three requests at a time, and the page then checks the results' revisions for later edits. Your question never leaves this device, but Wikipedia does learn which articles and sections your results are in. Wikipedia's text is not stored by this site.
